## Part 4: Engagement Strategy by Account Type

### If You Find a Founder Tweeting About Pain Points:

**Step 1 (Day 1-2):**
- Retweet with a thoughtful comment
- Example: "This is so true. Been helping teams solve this with [your method]"

**Step 2 (Day 3-4):**
- Reply to their tweets (genuine responses, not promotional)
- Join conversations where they're tagged

**Step 3 (Day 5-7):**
- Send warm DM: "Hey [name], loved your thread about [topic]. I work with founders on this exact problem..."

---

### If You Find Someone Asking "Looking for a Designer/Developer":

**Immediate Action (within 30 min):**
- Reply in thread with portfolio link
- Example: "Hey! I work with [target audience] on exactly this. Here's similar work: [portfolio link]"
- Keep it short and helpful

**If They Reply:**
- Move to DM quickly
- Get on call within 24 hours
- Budget for paid call if possible
