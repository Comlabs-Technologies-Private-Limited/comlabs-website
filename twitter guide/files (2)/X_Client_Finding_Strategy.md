# How to Find Clients on X (Twitter) for Web Dev, SaaS, Website Design & Digital Marketing

## Overview
X is a goldmine for finding clients actively looking for web development, SaaS solutions, website design, and digital marketing services. People frequently tweet "I'm looking for a web designer" or "I need help with my website" — you just need to know where to search and how to engage.

4. Set up searches daily or multiple times per week

## Strategy 5: DM Outreach Framework

### When to Send DMs:
- After you've engaged with their content 3-5 times
- When they tweet about a pain point you solve
- When they ask questions in public

### DM Template That Works:

**Hi [Name],**

**I noticed you tweeted about [specific problem]. I've helped [number] [target audience] with exactly this.**

**Quick example: [brief case study or result]**

**Would be happy to share more if helpful. Or no worries if not your focus right now.**

**[Your name]**

### Key Points:
- Personalize with their name and specific tweet reference
- Keep it short (under 3 sentences)
- Focus on THEIR problem, not your service
- Offer value, not a pitch
- No links in first message

## Red Flags to Avoid

❌ **Don't:**
- Spam with sales pitches
- Use generic "Here to help" messages
- Post without engaging first
- Ignore comments on your tweets
- Send DMs to accounts you've never engaged with
- Over-promote (should be 20% promotion, 80% value)

✅ **Do:**
- Engage authentically first
- Provide value before pitching
- Reply to every message
- Build relationships
- Be patient (2-3 week pipeline)
- Focus on helping others
